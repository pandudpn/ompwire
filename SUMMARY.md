## Every bot can now have its own soul 🎭

## The short version

- **Per-bot personas**: each gateway gets its own `SOUL.md` — described in one line at setup,
  drafted by an agent, approved once, then spoken from every turn after (#12).
- Telegram replies stop failing silently: outgoing messages are cut to the length Telegram
  actually counts instead of being rejected.
- Release notes now credit each entry's authors next to their links.
